Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our quote process

How long does it take to receive a quote?

We typically provide quotes within 2-3 business days after the initial consultation. For complex projects, it may take up to 5 business days to ensure accuracy.

Is there a fee for getting a quote?

No, we provide quotes free of charge. We believe in transparent pricing and helping you understand the investment required for your project before any commitment.

What information do I need to provide for an accurate quote?

The more details you provide, the more accurate your quote will be. Project type, dimensions, specific requirements, timeline, and budget range are especially helpful. Photos of the space and inspiration images are also valuable.

How detailed is the quote I'll receive?

Our quotes include a breakdown of services, materials, estimated timeline, and payment terms. We strive to be transparent and comprehensive so you know exactly what to expect.
